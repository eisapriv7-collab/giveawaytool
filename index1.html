CHETOPOLY

A full-3D Monopoly-style stream game. Cinematic camera rolls the dice, shows the number, then zooms in and follows each participant as they walk to their box. Every tile's image and text is editable.
VERSION 1

Race to the Finish

First participant to reach the GO finish line wins. Community Chest earns chat points, Chance sends you forward/back, Jail skips a turn.

 VERSION 2

Rolls-to-Prize

Configurable 2 / 3 / 5 rolls to reveal a prize. Double rolls go again. Two dice, top of 12 spaces. First to GO takes the top prize. Fully customisable prize tiles.
