How to Play

1

Two random entrants are paired up each round. An absurd scenario appears (e.g., "Who would survive a zombie apocalypse?").

2

Chat has 20 seconds to vote by typing !left or !right — majority decides the winner.

3

The winner advances to the next round. Loser is eliminated. Rounds repeat until one champion remains.

4

Works perfectly with stream delay — votes are locked in before the reveal. No reaction speed needed!
